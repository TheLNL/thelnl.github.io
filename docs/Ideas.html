This is a list of various ideas to explore.

Disclaimer: these ideas are raw thoughts without much consideration of fesability or consequences, they can and will be stupid at times.

1- Newspaper generator: Newspaper reading experience is much better than reading of articles on the internet or through RSS feed readers for some reason. So maybe we can mimic it by formatting rss feeds like newspapers.

2- Image recognition survey tool: Automatic tagging of various features of objects whose pics have been taken so that systematic survey can be done faster than manually entering values

3- Legal text link database: you can run OCR through the archives of law gazettes and extract metadata like references to other law, connection of all amendments to the original law, etc... This can then be used to make a db of law that people can go through, drop comments on issues, identify contradictions, suggest fixes, etc...

4- What if we had active RTI ? instead of people individually requesting certain docs periodically, with a higher threshold one can legally demand that the govt should publish certain data whenever it collects it.

5- What if govts had paid issue finding bodies whose job is to go around and find issues instead of relying on the citizens to report it on an app ?

6- Identify incorrectly marked bus stops by watching live bus data and recording where buses actually stop.
